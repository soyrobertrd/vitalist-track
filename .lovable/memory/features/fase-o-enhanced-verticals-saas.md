## New DB Tables Added
- `sillones_dentales` — Dental chairs/stations
- `comisiones_profesional` — Doctor commissions by procedure type
- `presupuestos_sonrisa` — Smile quotes (PSR-) with before/after photos, digital signature
- `recordatorios_fidelizacion` — Loyalty reminders (limpieza 6m, reactivación, examen anual, promos, upgrades, cumpleaños, referidos)
- `cabinas_estetica` — Treatment cabins (facial/corporal/laser/cirugia/mixta)
- `membresias_estetica` — Client memberships (MBR-) with session tracking
- `fotos_evolucion` — Before/during/after evolution photos with consent
- `promociones_estetica` — Promotions with codes, discounts, usage tracking
- `reservas_recovery` — Room reservations (RSV-) with packages (basico_3/premium_5/premium_7/vip_10/custom)
- `alertas_emergencia_recovery` — Emergency alerts by severity
- `servicios_concierge` — Concierge services (airport, driver, translator, shopping)
- `combos_optica` — Optical bundles with regular vs combo pricing
- `garantias_optica` — Product warranties with claim tracking

## Sub-niches Covered
- **Dental**: General, Ortodoncia, Implantología, Odontopediatría, Endodoncia, Multi-doctor
- **Estética**: Cirugía, Medicina estética, Facial, Depilación láser, Corporal, Spa médico
- **Recovery**: Post-lipo/BBL, Premium turismo médico, Local económica, Domiciliarios, Enfermería privada
- **Óptica**: Barrio, Premium, Cadena multi-sucursal, Con examen visual, Centro oftalmológico
